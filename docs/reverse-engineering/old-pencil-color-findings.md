# Legacy Pencil native color and export controls

Four synthetic Drawing GLV1 host-Mesa controls retain complete 1920 × 1080 RGBA8 frames and native vertex buffers: opaque red, half-alpha red on transparent pixels, half-alpha red over an opaque backdrop, and a pressure ramp over a two-color backdrop. The test-only model matches all four under twelve arithmetic profiles. The controls require per-instance byte-alpha composition: composing the completed transparent frame over the backdrop differs from the native background draw. Two translated pressure ramps also match all twelve profiles. These finite controls do not establish general interpolation or rasterization arithmetic.

Additional synthetic controls join native sampling and host pixels. A Fountain-origin serialized object altered to resolve Pencil produces 31 records from 204 points, matching an explicit size / 5 distance-gate profile; the original no-gate sampler produces 202. Tests supply the native-observed name and stroke type because the isolated payload decoder does not resolve them. Four size-8 color controls match a test-only F16 round-toward-zero atlas-alpha hypothesis, but final shader machine arithmetic remains unverified. These results do not establish automatic admission of real saved Pencil notes.

The opt-in direct PDF profile remains an approximation. Its luminosity-mask Forms improve aggregate error for the tested admitted controls in MuPDF and PDFium, including size-12 original, reflected and rapid-bend trajectories. Changed-pixel counts and maximum channel errors can increase even when aggregate error falls. Transparent and translucent controls remain pixel-inexact; arbitrary backdrops and size-12 transparent-prior behavior are unverified. Controlled repeated-stroke and two-color overlap captures do not establish production scheduling or general overlap fidelity. All tested 2× projections are host interventions, not saved-page zoom; reflected or altered trajectories may retain stale stored bounds.

Size 5 remains on PDF fallback. Although two multi-stamp controls improved with diagnostic direct export, a stationary size-5 dot improved MuPDF aggregate RGB error from 429 to 346 while worsening PDFium prior-relative error from 475 to 590. Those controls do not justify broader admission.

Size-12 single-stamp PDF inputs also remain on fallback. An earlier visible-dot comparison used pressure 0.5 instead of the native input's 0 and is invalid. The corrected diagnostic improves aggregate error for that dot, but the original-phase red-on-green dot regresses at 1× in both readers: MuPDF 3,576→4,303 and PDFium 3,550→4,445. Results change with phase and projection. Synthetic SVG dot comparisons improve error, but separately compositing rendered layers over native priors does not establish in-process canvas behavior.

## Retired PDF and SVG diagnostics

Temporary diagnostic exporters, PDF mutation probes and their comparators have been retired. Analytic type-1 shading worsened aggregate and maximum error in both readers on every tested control; shifting an image mask helped some controls and worsened others. Neither established a general correction. Resolution-specific per-pixel Forms reduced error but depended on the chosen pixel grid and were not scalable native vector geometry. These are historical conclusions, not available replay workflows.

[Color capture instructions](../../tools/old-pencil-color/README.md) and [translated capture instructions and maintained reader checks](../../tools/old-pencil-counterexample/README.md) preserve the tracked reproduction workflow. Additional local capture scaffolding is outside that workflow. Reader measurements used PyMuPDF 1.28.2 and PDFium 150.0.7869.0 through pypdfium2 5.9.0; the maintained oracles retain detailed expectations for current profiles. Native recapture requires local APK libraries, runtime helpers and host Mesa dependencies whose complete closure is not packaged. These synthetic host controls establish neither device, real saved-note nor general cross-reader parity.
