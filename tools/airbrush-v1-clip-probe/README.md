# AirBrush V1 triangle clip experiment

This historical isolated experiment replaced the three gradient masks on each image triangle with a geometric SVG clip path. It kept the per-triangle texture transforms. The retained synthetic Model/Drawing host-Mesa frames and stamp streams are the targets; these are not device or saved-note evidence. This change was rejected: it degraded all three resvg frames and most PDF metrics. The source patch also affected V2; the existing `saved_v2_tool1_native_motion_exports` test failed its native-frame hash. The scale2 patch had the incidental V2 tool-sampling change described in the scale2 README, so the V2 failure is evidence of a regression in the combined experiment, not isolation of its cause.

Against native, in order resvg premultiplied RGBA, MuPDF premultiplied RGBA, PDFium alpha and white RGB absolute errors: zigzag 541, 7393, 2490, 4305 (baseline 0, 6952, 2329, 4063); reverse 259, 2429, 826, 1325 (baseline 0, 2216, 818, 1325); scaled reverse 579, 6166, 2241, 3543 (baseline 0, 5421, 2216, 3646). Baselines are from the tool1 and scale2 receipts. Geometry bytes match native on all three controls. The frame and vector inputs are retained in those receipts.

The patches and comparator for revision `417cab5c` have been retired. Native inputs remain in `tools/pen-capture/airbrush_v1_tool1_receipt.json` and `tools/airbrush-v1-tool1-scale2/receipt.json`; see their owning READMEs for native recapture. The historical comparison used resvg-generated SVG PNGs and direct scene PDFs with PyMuPDF 1.28.2, Pillow 12.1.1 and PDFium 150.0.7869.0 at 96 dpi. Other scales and ordered scenes remain untested.
