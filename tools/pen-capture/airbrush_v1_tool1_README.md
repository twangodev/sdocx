# AirBrush V1 tool 1 diagnostic

This is an experimental candidate, not a production profile. The receipt preserves two synthetic Model/Drawing host-Mesa captures, including original lower-left RGBA frames, source arrays, vector bytes, hashes, and capture prerequisites. It does not establish saved-note or device parity. The capture script requires the original hash-pinned local native runner and dependencies; they are not supplied by a clean checkout. The original capture script hash remains in the receipt; the separate replay script hash records its later exclusive-output change. Do not regenerate the native receipt from reader output.

The historical candidate used revision `5cc39d4cc3e84d99a88da398c1404ec23deb8331`. Its patches and export comparators have been retired. PyMuPDF 1.28.2 and Pillow 12.1.1 compared 1×, 2× and 4× renders, box-reduced per channel, with the original premultiplied native frame. This reader sampling experiment did not change the PDF or prove parity at other scales. `airbrush_v1_tool1_verify.py` retains native-capture verification and checks pinned MuPDF/PDFium controls when their dependencies and historical exports are available.

At 1x MuPDF, the reverse candidate increases RGBA error from 1,558 to 2,216. At 2x and 4x its total error falls below generic, but its alpha error remains higher. The candidate therefore remains diagnostic only; the exact host SVG matches do not resolve the reader regression.

A separate historical raster transport diagnostic generated PNGs from sampled source geometry and texture; native frames were comparison targets only. It measured MuPDF 1.28.2 and PDFium 150.0.7869.0 at 96 dpi on isolated 256×256 controls. Scene ordering, other output scales, saved notes and devices remain unverified; no production profile was enabled. Native captures and compact receipts remain retained.
